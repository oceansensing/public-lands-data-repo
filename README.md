# public-lands-data-repo

The United States' public lands and the Forest Service's roads and trails, as
vector tiles for the oceansensing.org ocean map: PAD-US 4.1's protected areas
with their public access, manager, designation and GAP status, and the Motor
Vehicle Use Map's roads and trails with the vehicles each is open to and their
dates, beside every National Forest System trail and the uses it is managed
for.

Updated daily; PAD-US is rebuilt when USGS's service changes, the routes each
month.

## Sources and credits

**PAD-US 4.1**, USGS Gap Analysis Project, Protected Areas Database of the United States, published 2025-03-31: public domain. Its caveats travel with it: not a legal boundary; public access assigned largely by designation type; not every area reviewed locally; and, by its use constraints, not to be combined with finer data into hybrid maps — so it is published as USGS publishes it. Citation: U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) Gap Analysis Project (GAP), 2024, Protected Areas Database of the United States (PAD-US) 4.1: U.S. Geological Survey data release, https://doi.org/10.5066/P96WBCHS.

**The Forest Service's roads and trails**, its Geodata Clearinghouse, refreshed weekly: no access constraint; not legal documents, not to determine title, ownership, boundaries, jurisdiction or restrictions; altered downloads shared are to be named as such. Not every National Forest publishes Motor Vehicle Use Map data, and an open date is when a vehicle is legal, not when a road is passable.
